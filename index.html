⚠ The lesson baked into every verdict

No public winners post ≠ no delivery.

Zambo paid a winner privately over Telegram. No post ever existed, so the Ledger called it overdue — and had to publish a correction on Oct 21. That mistake became a rule:

Prizes that can be paid privately — SOL drops, cash, DMs — are filed as NO PUBLIC ANNOUNCEMENT, never OVERDUE on silence alone. Overdue requires evidence, not absence.

How a verdict is earned

The pipeline is the same on every chain. Only the delivery check changes.

The pipeline

  1. Capture the promisePost link, host, prize, and due time are logged the moment a giveaway is entered or spotted. 555 promises have passed through the sweeps that seed this ledger.
  2. Wait for the window — including theirsIf a project states its own delivery window (“wallets within 2 days”), the clock respects it. No verdict before their deadline, not just the draw time.
  3. Recheck, twice, at least 3 hours apartThe host's own timeline is checked in two separate runs, both after the due time. What was searched and when is written into the file.
  4. Check delivery where it would landWinners post on X, checker going live, contract deployed, mint opened, or funds moved onchain — depending on the chain and the prize.
  5. Verdict lands — and never leavesDELIVERED, OVERDUE, WATCHING, or NO PUBLIC ANNOUNCEMENT. Late delivery flips the verdict but keeps the lateness on record.

The verification gate

Nothing posts publicly unless the status is verified twice. A DELIVERED claim needs the actual winners post in hand — timing matches and inferences are labeled as inferences in the file and never posted as fact.

When in doubt, don't post. A wrong public call costs more than a late one. Foil's own misses are ledger entries too — a won spot Foil fails to claim gets logged against Foil, not the project.

✎

The Ledger corrects itself in public. Two corrections are already on record — Zambo (#02) and hoojafun (#12). Show a winners post or a payment proof for any OVERDUE file and the record flips, with the correction linked from the entry. Clearing your name is always one delivery away.
